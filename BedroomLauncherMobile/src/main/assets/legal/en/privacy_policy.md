# Bedroom Launcher Privacy Policy

**Project:** Bedroom Launcher  
**Developer:** voltage!studios  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective and last updated:** November 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bedroom Launcher stores, processes, and transmits information when you use the app on Android.

Bedroom Launcher is an unofficial launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition. It may access local device storage, game files, downloaded content, account-sign-in flows, and third-party services depending on the features you use.

We do not operate a centralized user-account system for the launcher itself, and the core local features can work without creating a separate account with us. However, when you sign in with Microsoft or use online services, those providers may receive the information required to authenticate you and serve game-related content.

We do not host or upload your logs to a public service. Logs remain on your device unless you intentionally export or share them yourself. By using Bedroom Launcher, you agree to this Privacy Policy and the app's User Agreement.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Bedroom Launcher may store or process the following locally:

- launcher preferences and settings;
- selected language, appearance, controls, layout choices, and UI configuration;
- installed game versions, Java runtimes, and runtime settings;
- mod directories, downloaded resources, saves, profiles, logs, and exported data;
- locally cached account metadata, such as usernames, player identifiers, OAuth tokens, and related sign-in data required for game access; and
- crash reports, diagnostics, performance traces, and other technical information generated by the app or game runtime.

This information stays on your device unless you deliberately export, copy, upload, or share it.

## 3. Account and Authentication Information

If you sign in with a Microsoft account, the app may receive and store the account information needed to complete sign-in, validate access, and launch game services. This can include identifiers, token data, player profile identifiers, and related metadata.

We do not collect or store your Microsoft password directly in the app. Authentication is handled through the provider's own sign-in flow and is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

If you use a third-party authentication server or custom login source, the server operator determines how that data is used and retained. We do not control those services and strongly recommend using only trusted providers.

## 4. Network Communication

Bedroom Launcher may communicate with third-party services when you:

- sign in to an account;
- download or check updates;
- retrieve game content or runtime components;
- access project information or support links;
- use multiplayer or room-based connectivity features; or
- interact with external providers selected by you.

These requests may involve your device's IP address, request metadata, time of access, URL, and related technical information. Third-party services may record this data according to their own privacy and retention practices.

We do not control those services' logs, retention periods, or security practices.

## 5. Download Sources, Mods, and External Content

Bedroom Launcher may fetch game files, launchers, Java runtimes, mod loaders, mods, resource packs, shaders, and other content from official and community sources.

The operators of those sources may receive file requests, version selections, architecture details, user-selected identifiers, and related metadata. We do not guarantee the privacy, safety, or legality of any third-party content source.

Downloaded or imported content can also make its own network requests after installation. Those communications are not controlled by us and are governed by the source content's own policies.

## 6. Diagnostics, Logs, and Crash Data

To help diagnose issues, the app and the game may generate local logs, crash reports, and technical records. These may include:

- app and game version information;
- device model, Android version, and system details;
- runtime, graphics, and compatibility information;
- errors, stack traces, connection details, or player identifiers; and
- file paths or configuration entries relevant to the issue.

These logs remain local by default. We do not upload them to a public or project-hosted service. If you choose to export or share a log manually, the receiving party will receive the full contents of that file.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You can control many data-handling choices directly from your device or app settings, including:

- deleting local logs or cached files;
- revoking account access or tokens where supported by the provider;
- disabling features you no longer use; and
- exporting or preserving data only when you explicitly choose to do so.

If you are no longer using a third-party account, we recommend revoking access through the provider's account settings and removing any stored local data from the app.

## 8. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to a local Android launcher, but no method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free. You are responsible for securing your device and account credentials.

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the app's functionality, support troubleshooting, or comply with legal requirements. Any data stored in third-party services is governed by those providers' own retention policies.

## 9. Children and Minors

Bedroom Launcher is intended for users who are legally permitted to use Minecraft and related services in their jurisdiction. If you are under the age required by law or by the relevant service provider, use the app only with parental or guardian consent and supervision.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes or as legal, technical, or service requirements evolve. When an update is made, the latest version will be shown in-app before continued use. Continued use of the app after an update indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions, privacy requests, or security concerns, contact the project maintainers through the official project channels or repository information.
